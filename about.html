About PTE AI Score Predictor

Empowering PTE Academic test-takers worldwide with smart score estimation tools.

Our Mission

At PTE AI Score Predictor, our goal is to bring clarity to the Pearson Test of English (PTE Academic) scoring system. Because PTE uses automated machine-scoring across cross-module tasks, calculating your estimated target score can often feel confusing. We designed our platform to deliver fast, transparent, and accurate score calculations for student practice sessions.

Key Benefit: Our algorithm reflects current weighting models across all four communicative skills—Speaking, Writing, Reading, and Listening.

What We Offer

Our web platform provides two dedicated tools:

  • Quick Score Check: Input your overall module marks to receive instant average score distributions.
  • AI Advanced Analysis: Input your individual task accuracy percentages (e.g., Read Aloud, Write From Dictation, Repeat Sentence) to evaluate section-by-section weightings based on Pearson's cross-module scoring rules.

Independent Preparation Tool

This platform was created as a free, accessible resource for candidates aiming for higher education admissions, skilled migration points, or professional registrations in destinations such as Australia, the UK, Canada, and New Zealand.
